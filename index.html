Frequently asked questions

I bought something but did not receive it.
Open the shop and tap Restore purchases at the bottom. If that does not help, email us with the date of the purchase.

I got a new phone. How do I get my purchases back?
Sign in with the same Apple ID, open the shop and tap Restore purchases. One-time items (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Ultimate soldiers) come back. Jade, Warbucks and lives are used up when you spend them and cannot be restored.

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

How do I get a refund?
Payments are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
In the game, open the sound menu and tap Privacy settings. You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
